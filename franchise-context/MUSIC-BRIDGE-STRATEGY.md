# 🎵 THE MUSIC BRIDGE — Which song gets teased (and how)

> **Your worry:** *"putting the songs out without the series will associate the songs with Mia's books and not the series."*
> That's a real branding risk — and the fix is to make the association happen **on purpose**, in-story, pointing AT the series. This doc maps it.

---

## The problem, stated simply

- **Laughter Currency** = Solani's song. **Headlines** = the series finale song.
- If either gets *released* alongside Mia's books, buyers will file it under "Mia's world."
- Mia's books and the series share a universe but are different lanes — the music must belong to the **series lane**, and Mia's books should *point* to it, not own it.

## The solution: a 3-act music architecture

```
ACT 1 — Laughter Currency (TEASED, never released)
   Mia Season 1–2: Mia overhears her big sister Solani writing a song in her
   room — fragments of lyrics, a melody through the door. It's Solani's
   private "hiding" song. Mia doesn't understand it yet. Neither does the reader.

ACT 2 — Headlines (TEASED, never released)
   Mia Season 2 FINALE: the school talent show. Mia sits in the crowd,
   watching Solani and her friends on stage. The catchy part of "Headlines"
   plays for a few lines. Then Mia turns to the READER and does THE HANDSHAKE:
   "Solani! Tell them how it all started!" / "Tell who?" / "Them."
   → the baton passes to the SERIES, with the reader handed over like family.
   Full scripted beat: `READER-HANDSHAKE-BEAT.md`

ACT 3 — Headlines (FULL RELEASE)
   Series Book 10, the finale. The same talent-show scene returns — but now
   the reader knows everything, and the full song lands with total payoff.
```

**Why this works:**
- The songs are *introduced* in Mia's books but clearly labeled **"that's my big sister's song"** — association built by design, pointing at the series
- Neither song is ever *released* before its moment — the actual audio drops only with the series (Laughter Currency with Part 1, Headlines with Part 2)
- The Mia Season 2 finale and the series prologue become the **same scene from two angles** — the books literally hand the baton
- No one can confuse the lanes: Mia's books TEASE, the series RELEASES

## What Mia's books get so they're not silent

**Option: a tiny "Mia jingle"** — her vlog stinger, 10 seconds, made with your existing pipeline (a Mia poem → Pattison → Suno). Cheap, clearly Mia's, and it means every lane has its own sound:

| Lane | Music |
|---|---|
| Mia's primer (2 seasons) | Mia's jingle (hers) + teases of Solani's songs (pointing up) |
| The series Part 1 | Laughter Currency released — where Solani STARTS |
| The series Part 2 | Headlines released at the finale — where she becomes |

## Decision to make later (not urgent)
1. Do you want a **Mia jingle** at all, or keep Mia's books silent except for the in-story teases? (Recommendation: yes to the jingle — it's 1 day of pipeline work.)
2. **Laughter Currency teaser placement:** overheard-through-the-door moment in Season 1, or save all music for Season 2? (Recommendation: Season 1 gets a tiny hummed melody; Season 2 gets lyric fragments.)
3. **Headlines teaser length:** just the chorus hook at the talent show, nothing more. The full song is the B10 crown jewel. 🔒

### ENTRY 001 — Fine Motor / Scissor Skills Seasonal — May 2026

**Product family:** Fall Scissor Skills Cutting Practice
**Product code:** SSP-FM-001 (Fall)
**Research date:** 2026-05-01
**Researched by:** Scout

**Primary search queries used:**
- "fall scissor skills cutting practice kindergarten"
- "fall cutting practice preschool printable"
- "color cut glue fall prek kindergarten"

**Observations from TPT search (do not treat these as verified sales data — these are observations at the time of research):**
- Multiple sellers offer fall cutting practice — market is active
- Most top results are large TPT stores with 1000+ products
- Left-handed versions are rarely featured as a selling point by competitors
- Skill progression (simple to complex) is not commonly called out in competitor listings
- Price range observed: approximately $3.00–$5.50 for individual packs of 10–20 pages

**Keyword gap identified:**
Left-handed cutting practice is an underserved differentiator. Competitors show worksheets but do not lead with the inclusion story. SSP's "Right + Left Versions" feature can own this differentiator if leading with it in the title and Slot 03.

- "scissor skills fall"
- "color cut glue fall kindergarten"
- "fall fine motor kindergarten"
- "fall cutting practice preschool"
- "left handed scissor skills"

**Recommended primary keyword:** fall scissor skills cutting practice kindergarten

**Recommended secondary keywords:**
- fall fine motor practice
- color cut glue activities
- left handed cutting practice
- fall centers preschool
- morning tubs fall
- preschool cutting practice printable

**Price range observed:** $3.00–$5.50

**SSP competitive advantage:** SSP is one of the few sellers explicitly offering both right-handed and left-handed versions in the same pack at the same price. This is a genuine product differentiator that can command the mid-to-upper end of the price range.

## RESEARCH GUIDELINES FOR SCOUT

1. **Do not fabricate data.** Never invent download counts, review numbers, or prices. If a piece of data is not directly observable, note it as "not visible" rather than guessing.

2. **Date everything.** Keyword trends shift. Research from 6 months ago may not reflect current buyer behavior. All entries must have a research date.

3. **Be specific about the gap.** "SSP is better" is not a gap. "Left-handed cutting practice is mentioned in fewer than 20% of top 10 results for 'fall cutting practice kindergarten'" is a gap.

4. **Separate observations from recommendations.** What was observed goes in the observations section. Scout's recommendation goes in the recommendation section. Owner approves recommendations.

5. **Update entries when relaunching.** If a product is updated or relaunched, run new keyword research and add a new entry. Do not rely on research that is more than 12 months old for active products.

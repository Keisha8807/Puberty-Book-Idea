## QA gates

Each board must pass:

- Exact approved face/age/hair identity
- No extra limbs or impossible anatomy
- Front/three-quarter/side/back consistency where requested
- Outfit and prop continuity
- No unauthorized text, logos, or false factual content
- Clear expression differentiation
- Keisha approval before moving from candidate to approved reference

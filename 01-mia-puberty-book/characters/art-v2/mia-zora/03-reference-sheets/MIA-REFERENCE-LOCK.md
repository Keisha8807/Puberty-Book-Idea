## Immutable identity

- 10-year-old African American Black girl; must read clearly as 10, never younger-child or teen
- Warm brown skin with golden undertones; preserve dimensional lighting rather than reducing skin to one flat swatch
- Large expressive brown eyes
- Light freckles across nose and upper cheeks
- Bright even front teeth with **no gap and no braces**
- Natural dark hair in two high fluffy puffs with neat braided/cornrow front sections
- Colorful star clips: purple/yellow family on viewer-left side; yellow/coral family on viewer-right side, with tiny side clips as shown
- Small gold hoop earrings
- Stylized child proportions from the approved turnaround; no adult curves or teen proportions

## Signature hero outfit

- Bright multicolor paint-splash t-shirt
- Hot-pink overalls with small colorful flower pins
- Colorful beaded bracelets
- Matching multicolor low-top sneakers with pink toes and white laces

Outfit-of-the-day changes require a separate approved costume board. Her face, hair construction, star language, age, and proportions do not change.

## Core visual palette

- Hot pink
- Teal
- Aqua
- Sunny yellow
- Coral
- Lavender
- Warm cream
- Gold accents
- Deep natural hair brown

Use the approved palette strip and hero—not generic pastel stock art.

## Rendering rules

- Premium warm modern 3D animated feature-film finish
- Expressive face rig, dimensional skin, natural hair texture, fabric detail
- Warm lighting and vibrant color without whitening or oversaturating her skin
- No logos, copyrighted background art, garbled text, extra limbs, face drift, tooth gap, braces, hair beads, or substituted clips
- Approved hero always outranks a generated model board

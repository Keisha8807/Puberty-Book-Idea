## Required before approval

- [ ] Character and file are identified
- [ ] Source/platform is recorded
- [ ] Keisha generated it or otherwise has the right to use it
- [ ] It is not third-party franchise or character art
- [ ] Private-child likeness/voice permission is not an issue
- [ ] Highest-resolution original is retained when available
- [ ] Canon, age, style, and quality checks pass
- [ ] Keisha explicitly approves the identity

## Reference-use rule

A candidate can guide generation before final publication, but approval does not erase its source history. The final hero and reference sheet should preserve their prompt, source image(s), version, and approval record in the later Visual Factory log.

## 4. LEVEL 1 EPISODE FLOW CARDS — SEASON 2

### EPISODE 12 — HAIR TODAY, GONE TOMORROW

**Premise:** Body hair shows up differently on everyone; Mia hunts for one universal rule.

**Mia's want:** Find the correct rule and apply it before anyone notices.
**Hidden fear:** Choosing wrong means being judged or not belonging.
**Educational promise:** Body-hair variation is normal; removal/trimming/leaving are personal choices; discussable with trusted adults.
**Myth Machine theories:** Shaving is mandatory / hair = dirty / one rule fits all / talking about it makes it spread.
**Truth-givers:** Mema + Brenda.
**Stinger:** Bra-shopping flyer.

### EPISODE 13 — THE BRA-VO! MISSION

**Premise:** Bra shopping as identity navigation, not fix-it.

**Mia's want:** Prove she is correctly categorized.
**Hidden fear:** Visible development equals public judgment.
**Educational promise:** Bras are for comfort/support; bodies and needs vary; fit > fashion.
**Myth Machine:** One size/style fixes everything / bra = maturity badge / everyone needs same one.
**Truth-givers:** Brenda + Seraphina (rare kind beat) + Marcus camera as support.
**Stinger:** Jada's crush.

### EPISODE 14 — CRUSH CONFIDENTIAL

**Premise:** Crushes as practice, not promise.

**Mia's want:** Decode crush as permanent contract.
**Hidden fear:** New feelings threaten friendships or make her disloyal.
**Educational promise:** Crushes are common and varied; you can feel without promising; boundaries still apply.
**Myth Machine:** Crush = love forever / you must tell everyone / feelings equal action.
**Truth-givers:** Mema + Brenda.
**Stinger:** Shade Screen screenshot.

### EPISODE 15 — SELFIES, SNAPS & SHADE

**Premise:** Anonymous page tests boundaries in a new world.

**Mia's want:** Unmask the poster herself.
**Hidden fear:** If she doesn't control the narrative, it controls her.
**Educational promise:** Digital citizenship, privacy, reporting vs revenge, Uplift Project as allyship.
**Myth Machine:** Anonymous = invisible / internet is real life / you must watch it alone.
**Truth-givers:** Ms. Vitality + Brenda.
**Stinger:** Stress vortex — tests, growing, feelings.

### EPISODE 16 — THE STRESS VORTEX

**Premise:** Stress as whole-body signal, not weakness.

**Mia's want:** Power through alone.
**Hidden fear:** Asking for help = failing.
**Educational promise:** Stress and coping strategies; sleep/movement/talking help; pencil-charm as tactile coping anchor.
**Myth Machine:** Stress means weakness / worry alone helps / you should push through.
**Truth-givers:** Uncle Mo + Ms. Harmony.
**Stinger:** CROWN clue tease (clipboard unicorn backpack).

### EPISODE 17 — [FLOW GRID REBUILD — CROWN CLUE]

**Premise:** Solani's world whispers "crown" — Mia must decide not to solve it.

**Mia's want:** Solve Solani's membership/identity now.
**Hidden fear:** If she doesn't solve her sister, she'll be left behind.
**Educational promise:** Boundaries for curiosity; some stories belong to the person living them.
**Myth Machine:** Crown is literal headgear / you deserve access to every secret.
**Truth-giver:** Mema Hazel — boundary keeper.
**Stinger:** Talent show invitations.

### EPISODE 18 — THE TALENT SHOW (FINALE — READER HANDSHAKE)

**Premise:** Mia in the crowd, Solani on stage, the first chords of Headlines — then Mia turns to the reader.

**Mia's want:** Stay the narrator/main character forever.
**Hidden fear:** Handing off means disappearing.
**Educational promise:** Making room for others' voices; growth includes connection without needing to predict everything.
**Myth Machine:** Hand-off = erasure / you must hold every story.
**Truth-givers:** Brenda + Solani + ensemble.
**Final beat:** Reader Handshake — "That part? That's not my story to tell. She will." — tees CROWN Club Book 1. Headlines teased (not released).

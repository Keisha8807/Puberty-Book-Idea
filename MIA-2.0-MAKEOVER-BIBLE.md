## 3. THE VOICE RULES (Georgia Nicholson × The Proud Family × 2026)

1. **Fourth wall, ALWAYS.** "Yes, YOU. I see you reading this during silent reading time, thinking you're slick."
2. **Made-up words.** "Hormotional" (hormonal + emotional). "The Puberty Police." "Glow-Up Emergency." If Mia needs a word, she invents it.
3. **Lists & bulletins.** "Signs of a Possible Glow-Up: Item 1..." — every investigation gets a case file.
4. **Dramatic overreaction, instant deflation.** Mia declares a national emergency; Mema corrects her with one line.
5. **Text threads + vlog segments.** The book breaks format like a show breaks the fourth wall.
6. **10-year-old logic, zero irony.** The comedy comes from how *seriously* she takes everything.

## 4. THE COMEDY ENGINE: THE MYTH MACHINE 🔥

**This is the facelift's biggest trick.** The stiffest part of the old book — the factual sections — becomes the funniest part.

- Mia, Jada & Emma run **The Myth Machine**: every chapter, they generate 3–4 *hilariously wrong* theories about the topic
- The book's myth-vs-fact sections = **the machine's output**, busted by Mema Hazel / Solani / Ms. Vitality with a gag
- **The reader learns BY watching Mia get it wrong and corrected** — education becomes the punchline delivery system

> Example: "MYTH: Deodorant is armpit perfume for fancy people." — "FACT: Mema says sweat is normal, soap exists, and the only thing 'fancy' in this house is her Sunday hat."

**The old myth sections don't die — they get a job.** Every myth you already wrote becomes a Myth Machine card with a character reaction attached.

# 🤝 THE READER HANDSHAKE — the baton-pass beat

> The moment the whole universe is handed from Mia to Solani — and the reader is part of the scene.

---

## THE BEAT (Mia Season 2 finale → Series Prologue)

**INT. SCHOOL AUDITORIUM — TALENT SHOW — NIGHT**

The stage lights flare. Solani and the CROWN girls step into the glow — and the first chords of **HEADLINES** hit the room.

Mia, in the crowd, bounces in her seat. She grins at the stage... then turns and looks straight at US.

> **MIA:** "You picked the right night to show up. See her up there? The one with the butterfly clips? That's my sister, Solani."
>
> On stage, Solani grins into the mic — all confidence, all light.
>
> **MIA:** "She wasn't always like that, though. You should've seen her last year. Shoot — you should've seen *me* last year."
>
> The song swells. The chorus hits. Mia's eyes shine.
>
> **MIA:** "You know what? You need the whole story. The REAL one. From the beginning."
>
> She cups her hands around her mouth and yells over the music:
>
> **MIA:** "SOLANI! Tell them how it all started!"
>
> On stage, Solani hears her sister. She tilts her head.
>
> **SOLANI:** "Tell *who?*"
>
> Mia points at US. Directly at the reader. Like we're sitting right beside her.
>
> **MIA:** "Them."
>
> Beat.
>
> Solani looks straight down the barrel — at us. A slow, knowing smirk spreads across her face. The music keeps playing. She raises the mic.
>
> **SOLANI:** "Ohhh. *Them.*"
>
> **CUT TO BLACK.**
>
> **SOLANI (V.O.):** "Fine. But you better listen. 'Cause this isn't just my story..."

---

## WHY THIS IS FRANCHISE GOLD

1. **The reader is a character.** Both series now share one mechanic: *the narrator talks to you.* Mia talks to you in the primer. Solani talks to you in the series — because **Mia introduced you.** The handoff isn't just between books, it's between characters and the reader.

2. **"Them" = the CROWN value in action.** The reader isn't a stranger — they're a *real one*. Being brought in, addressed directly, seen — that's literally the club's whole message, delivered as craft.

3. **Solani gets her arc in one smirk.** On stage she's the Headlines girl (butterfly). But her "tell who?" — that flicker of surprise at being *seen* — that's the Laughter Currency girl still inside (caterpillar). The whole series lives between those two beats.

4. **It makes the Series Prologue free.** The series doesn't need to introduce itself — Solani's already holding the mic, talking to the reader Mia handed her. "Fine. But you better listen..." and the story starts.

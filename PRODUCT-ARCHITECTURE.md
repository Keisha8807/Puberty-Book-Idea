## THE THREE-AUDIENCE RULE (keeps everything clean)

| Audience | Gets | How |
|---|---|---|
| 👧🏾 **The girl** | The main books | **Story-first.** Education lives *inside* the story (Mia's factual sections lighten in the series; learning comes through the girls). No homework energy. |
| 👩🏽‍👧🏾 **Mom/caregiver + daughter** | The Companion Guide | **Syllabus-following, but warm.** Discussion prompts, "what your girl might be feeling," activities, myth-vs-fact, key terms — the curriculum with the clinical parts softened for the kitchen table. |
| 🏫 **The school** | The Teacher Syllabus Pack | **Cross-curricular lens cards.** One theme, seen through 6+ subjects, so *more than one teacher can participate.* |

## THE COMPANION QUESTION (pick one)

| Option | What it is | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Per-book companions** (10) | Each book gets its own thin guide | Maximum classroom fit | 10 more products to build |
| **B. Per-part companions** (2) | One guide per 5-book part | Matches your 2-part structure, half the work | Less granular |
| **C. Hybrid (recommended)** | 2 part companions + 1 primer companion + teacher lens cards | School depth via the cards, kitchen-table depth via the companions | 3 companions + cards |

My vote: **C.** The lens cards do the school granularity; the companions stay warm and readable for caregivers.

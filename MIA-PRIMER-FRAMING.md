## 3. THE FRAMING OPTIONS

### Option A — ALL-IN-ONE (current state, already designed)
Story + facts + myths in one book. Your Canva draft IS this.
- 💪 Girl reads it alone, everything in hand; one product; matches the original "part 1 story, part 2 clinical fun" vision
- ⚠️ Your own original fear: "they won't want to read a 300-page book"; chapters get heavy; inconsistent with the series decision ("education lives in the companion")

### Option B — STORY-FIRST + LIGHT EDUCATION (the hybrid-lite)
Keep story + myth/fact + a *"Mema Hazel's Wise Words"* sidebar per chapter. Move factual sections' deep content + glossary + quizzes to the companion.
- 💪 One book stays readable; education still peeks through; partially consistent with the series
- ⚠️ Splits your factual sections (they're written as full sections, not sidebars)

### Option C — STORY BOOK + COMPANION (mirrors the series exactly)
Mia story-only book + "Mia's Handbook" companion (facts, myths, glossary, true/false, activities — most of it EXISTS).
- 💪 100% consistent with your series architecture; two products = two channels (KDP book + TPT/Etsy companion); solves page count; the companion can bundle WITH the book
- ⚠️ A 5th grader reading solo doesn't get the education unless she has both; more assembly work

### Option D — SPLIT BY READER (my recommendation — hybrid of A+C)
**One book, two clear zones inside:**
- **Zone 1 (story):** Mia's story, light — with small "Fact Check" callouts woven in
- **Zone 2 (the deep stuff):** each chapter ends with a compact **"Mema Hazel Explains"** spread: the factual section + myths + 3 key terms — *readable in-book*
- **Separate companion:** only the interactive parts (true/false, quizzes, activities, journals) become "Mia's Glow Workbook" — sold alongside AND later as the TPT printable

Why D: it keeps the **solo-girl experience** complete (the primer's whole job — remember the origin story: your daughter wanted ONE book she could read alone), while still honoring the series rule *"keep the heavy curriculum out of the main books."* The workbook becomes the TPT/Etsy product and the companion for caregivers. And your Canva draft survives 90% — only the deep-interactive pages would shift to the workbook design.

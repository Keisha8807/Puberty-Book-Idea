## 0. The honest math before anything else

**Today is Thursday, August 20, 2026. Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026. That is 99 days.**

Three truths that shape every line below:

**Truth 1 — The graphic novel cannot be the Black Friday product.**
Canon v1.25 (DEC-043) locks interiors as a *full-color contemporary MG graphic novel*. A 9-episode full-color book is roughly 120–180 finished pages of consistent character art. Even with AI assist and your locked model boards, that is a 6–12 month art production, and every health line needs a human reviewer first. Promising it for November means shipping something thin with your name on it forever. **The graphic novel is a 2027 launch. That was already the plan — don't let Black Friday move it.**

**Truth 2 — You can still absolutely sell on Black Friday.** Just not that.
What *is* buildable in 99 days is a **digital-first Founding Reader offer**: low-art, high-value products that stand on your writing, your worksheets, your songs, and your voice — plus a paid slot in the 2027 book. See §1.

**Truth 3 — The real Black Friday prize is the list, not the revenue.**
Honest projection on a **$280 ad budget** and a from-scratch audience: the waitlist realistically reaches **~450–500 by November 26**, converting at 4–6% at a ~$25 average order — **roughly $450–$750 in Black Friday weekend sales.** That is a *good* outcome for a first-time author with no audience and under $300 of media spend, because you also walk into 2027 owning ~475 warm parent emails, three tested trailers, ad creative with real CPL data, Etsy/TPT listings with search history, and a finished spine. Judge the sprint on the list, the proof, and the assets. Revenue is a bonus.

With a small budget the levers that matter most are **free ones**: Pinterest search, Etsy/TPT's own buyer traffic, partner creators, and starting the waitlist **this week**. Adding more products does not help. Starting earlier does.

## 10. Paid ads — $280 budget, two concentrated bursts

**Objective:** waitlist signups. Not sales. A cold parent will join a free list; they will not buy a $39 bundle from a stranger.

**The rule small budgets live or die by: concentrate, don't dribble.** Meta needs roughly 50 conversions per week per ad set to exit its learning phase and optimize. $6/day forever never gets there — it just leaks money at bad prices. $10/day in short bursts does.

| Burst | Dates | Daily | Days | Spend | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Burst 1 — Find the winner** | Oct 12 – Oct 21 | $10 | 10 | $100 | 3 creatives that *already* performed organically, one audience, one objective (lead) |
| *(dark period)* | Oct 22 – Nov 8 | $0 | 18 | $0 | Organic + listings + partners. Bank the budget. |
| **Burst 2 — Fill the list** | Nov 9 – Nov 26 | $10 | 18 | $180 | Run the single winning creative into the waitlist right before BF |
| **BF weekend** | Nov 27 – Nov 30 | $0 | 4 | $0 | **Email your list instead** — free, and converts 10–50× better than cold ads |
| | | | | **$280** | |

**Rules:**
1. **Meta only** (Instagram + Facebook). Moms of 9–12s are there and targeting is best. At this budget, do not split across platforms — Pinterest gets your effort for free via SEO, not ad dollars.
2. **Never pay to test a creative organic hasn't already validated.** Your feed is a free testing lab. Ads amplify proven winners; they don't discover them.
3. **Kill fast:** anything over $3.00/lead by day 5 of Burst 1 dies. With $100 you can afford exactly one wrong answer.
4. **One objective, one audience, one landing page.** Complexity is what kills small budgets.
5. **Retarget inside Burst 2** if the pixel has 300+ page visitors by then — warm retargeting is usually the cheapest lead you'll buy.
6. **Watch one number:** cost per waitlist email. Target $1.00–$2.00. At $2.00, $280 buys ~140 emails; at $1.20, ~230.
7. 🔴 **Every budget change is yours.** No agent touches Ads Manager. Chrome may *read* the dashboard and write a report into the vault; it may not click.

### Where the rest of the list comes from (free)

Ads will supply maybe 150–230 of your ~475. The other half is earned:

| Free channel | Realistic contribution | Why it works at $0 |
|---|---|---|
| **Pinterest SEO** | 80–150 | Pins are *search results*, not posts — they keep pulling in traffic for months. Highest ROI free channel for printables/caregiver content. Start Block 1, not later. |
| **Etsy + TPT built-in search** | 40–80 | These platforms bring their own buyers. **List SKU 1 by early October** so the listings accrue ranking and reviews before BF. |
| **IG/TikTok organic** | 60–120 | Your face, 2×/week, 4 pillars |
| **Micro-influencers / partner creators** | 80–250 | ⭐ Your highest-leverage free channel. 60 targets across 8 archetypes, gifted not paid, 40% affiliate. One share from the right person beats the entire ad budget. Full playbook: `_workhorse/02-inbox/marketing/CREATOR-AND-ORGANIC-PLAYBOOK.md`. 🔴 you send every message |
| **Facebook mom groups** | 20–50 | Participate as a human for weeks before ever mentioning the book |

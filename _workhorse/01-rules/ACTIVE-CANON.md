## 6. Primer structure and voice

- Two seasons, 18 episodes total
- Season 1: 11 episodes
- Season 2: 7 episodes including the talent-show finale
- Mia talks directly to the reader; the reader is her case partner
- Mia uses lists, case files, text threads, invented words, dramatic logic, and stingers
- Mia is confidently wrong; the educational correction delivers the punchline
- Jada and Emma join Mia in the Myth Machine
- Adults bust myths with warmth, specificity, and humor
- No lecture-first textbook voice
- No humiliation, shame, fearmongering, or oversexualized framing

### Standard episode anatomy

1. Cold open
2. Story with Fact Check cutaways
3. Myth Machine escalation
4. Trusted-adult explanation delivered through character and scene
5. Ask Mema Hazel/Mema educational feature as approved for that episode format
6. “Next time on…” stinger
7. Companion-resource pointer when applicable

The exact Ask Mema Hazel page treatment must be approved during the Episode 1 format gate; workers may not add duplicate educational sections.

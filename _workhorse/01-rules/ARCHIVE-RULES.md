# ARCHIVE RULES

> The archive preserves Keisha's creative history without letting old decisions rewrite the active project.

## What counts as archive

- The 2024 Canva visual draft
- Old chapter manuscripts and factual sections
- Old ChatGPT conversations
- Retired series structures
- Old character and family canons
- Real-school versions of fictional school ideas
- Early CROWN/Secret Sisters/Glow-Up Girls development
- Superseded art and prompts
- Old launch plans that assume the Canva manuscript is nearly finished

## Archive firewall

Archived content may not enter an active work packet unless the packet explicitly states:

```text
Archive access: Allowed
Purpose: Idea mining / topic comparison / historical trace
Elements requested:
Elements forbidden:
```

Default is **Archive access: Not allowed**.

## Allowed archive uses

- Verify the project's development history
- Recover a topic Keisha wants reconsidered
- Compare an old idea with current canon
- Preserve origin, research, or business context
- Identify assets that could be rewritten from scratch
- Prevent accidental loss of Keisha's work

## Forbidden archive uses

- Treat old prose as the new manuscript baseline
- Reintroduce retired names, heritage, schools, ages, grades, or titles
- Copy an old factual claim without new verification
- Fill a missing scene automatically
- Assume “nothing gets thrown away” means everything remains canon
- Merge conflicting family or series versions
- Publish archived material without a fresh approval and production pass

## The Canva rule

The Canva draft is evidence of Keisha's earlier vision, not a manuscript to assemble.

Nothing transfers automatically:

- Not prose
- Not chapter order
- Not page layout
- Not character depiction
- Not educational framing
- Not medical claims

A topic may be reconsidered only through a new work request and written fresh in Mia 2.0's voice.

## Superseded art rule

- Keep superseded art for provenance unless storage policy requires otherwise.
- Clearly label it as superseded.
- Do not use it in public assets.
- Do not ask later generators to average approved and superseded faces.
- The approved reference sheet wins.

## Historical names

Historical documents may retain old names to preserve context. New summaries must label them as historical and must not repeat them without a retirement warning.

Examples: Glow-Up Girls, Mia Luz, Solani Maris, Maritza, Tío Mo, real school names.

## No destructive cleanup by default

Do not delete or rewrite source archives merely because they conflict with canon. Isolate and label them. Any deletion, move, or large reorganization requires a separate approved work request.

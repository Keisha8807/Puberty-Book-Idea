# SAFETY AND APPROVALS

> The Workhorse prepares work. It does not replace Keisha, qualified reviewers, or responsible adult judgment.

## Risk levels

### GREEN — internal and reversible

Examples:

- Brainstorming
- Internal outlines
- File indexes
- Research queries
- Draft checklists
- Status summaries
- Format conversion

May run without a separate approval when the task stays inside an already approved work packet. Output must still be logged.

### YELLOW — Keisha approval required

Examples:

- Manuscript prose
- Plot or character recommendations
- Character art and prompts
- Educational explanations
- Marketing copy
- Freebies
- Website content
- Public-facing graphics
- Outreach drafts
- Calendar plans

Yellow work may be drafted automatically. It may not become canon, public, or final until Keisha approves it.

### RED — Keisha plus appropriate human/professional review

Examples:

- Medical and health claims for publication
- Personalized health or safety responses
- Child-submitted questions
- Legal, copyright, trademark, tax, or contract decisions
- Public school or organizational communications
- Spending, paid advertising, purchases, or financial-record changes
- Publishing/uploading final products
- Collecting information from children
- Public posts, direct messages, or email sends

Red work can be researched or drafted but cannot be executed automatically.

## Child-reader safety

- Do not shame, scare, sexualize, diagnose, or ridicule the reader.
- Do not solicit private stories, symptoms, photos, voice recordings, location, school, age/grade, or contact details directly from children.
- Do not create unmoderated child accounts, profiles, comments, DMs, or live chat.
- Public Ask Mema Hazel questions must be prewritten/approved or submitted through an adult-managed, reviewed process.
- Remove personal identifiers before any approved question is published.
- Serious health, abuse, self-harm, danger, or emergency concerns must direct the reader to a trusted adult and appropriate qualified help; the fictional character does not handle a crisis.

## Medical and educational accuracy

Every health-related production pass must:

1. Identify each factual claim.
2. Cite credible and current sources.
3. Separate general education from individualized advice.
4. Flag uncertainty and source disagreement.
5. Use age-appropriate language.
6. Receive qualified human review before publication.
7. Record the reviewer and version reviewed.

Never label someone a medical reviewer without their explicit consent.

## Cultural care

- Black girls remain centered.
- Cultural specificity must come from story truth and care—not a stereotype checklist.
- AI review cannot replace Keisha or human Black-girl/parent reader feedback.
- Do not add generic inclusivity language that weakens the stated mission.
- Do not speak for every Black family.

## Public-action restrictions

Without Keisha's explicit final approval, no worker may:

- Post or publish
- Send email or DMs
- Contact creators, schools, libraries, businesses, or organizations
- Submit forms
- Buy tools, ads, domains, services, or products
- Commit to dates, deliverables, partnerships, or events
- Change live listings or website settings
- Release songs
- Upload a book

## AI-production rules

- Keisha is the author and final creative authority.
- AI assistance may support organization, drafting, revision, research, art, and production.
- Do not claim AI output was reviewed by a human or professional when it was not.
- Follow platform disclosure requirements accurately.
- Preserve prompt, source, version, and approval records for production assets.
- Never clone a child's voice or use a child's image/voice without the necessary adult permission and a separate approved plan.

## Approval-gate design

- Present one decision at a time.
- Explain what the decision unlocks.
- Offer a focused correction path, not several unrelated options.
- “No response” never equals approval.
- Approval applies only to the version reviewed.
- Any material change creates a new version and approval gate.

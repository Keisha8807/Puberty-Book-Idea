# Closed Door

**Job:** Sister-reveal motif. Mostly instrumental.  
**Trailers:** #6 Character Reveal / Solani’s Door  
**Canon lock:** This is **not** Solani’s secret in-story song. Keep her thread muffled (E1 / E3 / E11).

**Style:** Mostly instrumental tween neo-soul cinematic cue, warm Black American R&B influence, intimate Rhodes, deep soft bass, restrained pocket drums, muted guitar, airy humming, respectful and reflective rather than sad, minimal young female vocal phrases, no full verses

## Proposed lock (if approved)

**FORMAT:** Full-color contemporary middle-grade graphic novel  
**STRUCTURE:** 11 episodic chapters forming one cumulative season arc  
**VISUAL DEVICE:** Comic panels + occasional full/half-page case files, diagrams, Myth Machine overlays, handwritten notes, vlogs, dossier pages  
**ART DIRECTION:** Expressive 2D digital cartooning with animation-level character acting, warm saturated color, strong Black hair/skin rendering, cinematic composition  
**TONE:** Comedy-forward coming-of-age, emotionally sincere when needed  
**MARKET POSITION:** Story-first MG graphic fiction with embedded puberty/SEL value — not an illustrated handbook  
**DIFFERENTIATOR:** Puberty mystery series; education emerges from investigations and consequences

Pitch line:

> A humorous, character-driven middle-grade graphic novel about Black girlhood and early puberty, combining an ongoing sister mystery with Myth Machine investigations, friendship stories, and visually integrated puberty information.

## Where 3D still belongs (if interiors go 2D)

Character development art, promotional renders, trailers, social, animation concepts.

## Interior look (if approved)

Clean expressive linework + rich warm color + slightly exaggerated facial acting + readable silhouettes + dynamic panels + gorgeous Black skin and textured hair.

Not photorealism. Not superhero anatomy. Not manga imitation. Not a 3D-render-per-panel book.

Think: **animated-series energy translated into comics.**

## Information pages

Do **not** auto-cut to a full-page infographic when an adult explains something.

Make info part of Mia’s system:

- MYTH MACHINE: CASE STATUS (wrong stamp → actual evidence)
- MIA'S EXTREMELY NECESSARY PERIOD EMERGENCY KIT v1.0 (ridiculous items crossed out)
- Feelings Forecast that starts absurd and gets truthful

Avoid: funny problem → adult lecture → lesson learned.

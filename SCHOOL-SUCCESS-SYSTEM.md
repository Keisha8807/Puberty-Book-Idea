## THE OFFERING (what "all in" means, tier by tier)

### Tier 1 — THE BOOKS (entry)
- **Primer** (5th grade) + **Part 1** & **Part 2** (7th grade) sold as classroom sets
- Part bundles = schools buy 5 books at once, not 10 decisions
- Every book: story-first, zero "textbook energy" — the hook that wins principals AND kids

### Tier 2 — THE TEACHER SYLLABUS PACK (the differentiator)
- **Cross-curricular lens cards per part**: one book theme seen through Health · Science · Social Studies · Arts · Media Literacy · Counseling/SEL · PE (your 7 existing syllabi)
- **Multi-teacher participation built-in** — a school can run "CROWN Week" where 6 teachers teach the same theme through their lens
- CASEL/SEL + health-standards alignment language (schools need to justify purchases)
- Low-prep: one page per lens per book, QR codes to extras

### Tier 3 — THE CROWN CLUB IN-A-BOX (the moat)
- Charter, meeting structure, rituals, and member activities from **CROWN + Ten to Me™**
- The **5-value × 2-cycle curriculum** = a ready-made club calendar for the school year
- Character "lanes" for activities (music/art/socials) — kids join the lane that fits them
- Pair with **The CROWN Club Creative Contest**: poems, art, songs via Sociamonials, voted by families — a school-wide event in a box

### Tier 4 — THE MUSIC + EVENTS (the magic no other puberty book has)
- **Laughter Currency + Headlines** as classroom hooks and assembly anthems
- The talent-show finale = an actual school event template (mirrors the book!)
- **The Growth Games** (your Feb 2025 concept) as an end-of-year reflection celebration
- Author visits (virtual first, then TOAST 🤫) + a school launch kit

### Tier 5 — THE EQUITY LAYER (Sunray Rising™)
- **Buy One, Glow One™**: every classroom set purchased donates a set to an under-resourced school
- Grant-ready mission language: literacy + SEL + representation + period equity
- The Imani storyline (period poverty) makes the books themselves the case for the program

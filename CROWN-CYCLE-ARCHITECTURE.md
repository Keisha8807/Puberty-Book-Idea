## The two-cycle structure

**5 values × 2 cycles = 10 months = 10 books.** The second time through, they're *stronger* in each area. Your blueprint already labels Books 6–9 with "(Revisited)".

```

─────────────────────────────          ─────────────────────────────────────
B1  Crowns in the Making                B6  Silent Strength
    OWNERSHIP  "My body, my voice,          WORTH (Revisited)  self-worth &
```

## The arc this creates (caterpillar → butterfly)

- **Cycle 1** = the girls meet their values the way beginners do — awkward, half-understood, tested
- **Cycle 2** = the same values return through harder battles — and the girls handle them *differently* because they've grown
- **B10** = the talent show. They perform **"Headlines"**. Teaser in the B1 prologue, full song as the payoff. Laughter Currency (hiding) → Headlines (seen).
